5. INTERPRETATION & METHODOLOGICAL NOTES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Key Findings:
  - Overall Predictive Power: Calibrated ESP strongly correlates with measured hardware performance
    across the full 24-cell dataset (Pearson r = +0.8468, Spearman rho = +0.8254).
  - Benchmark Divergence:
      * GHZ (r = +0.9958) and Mirror (r = +0.9368) exhibit strong positive tracking.
      * BV (r = -0.9904) exhibits an inverted correlation: because BV is extremely shallow (2 CX gates),
        gate errors are negligible and readout errors dominate; random layout achieved lowest gate depth but higher readout penalty.
      * QFT (r = +0.6086) shows moderate correlation, where compilation gate count (30 vs 41 CX) dominates.
  - Calibration Window Limitation:
      * All n = 24 cells were executed on a single calibration day (ibm_marrakesh, 9 Oct 2026).
      * Correlations reflect spatial variation across qubits rather than longitudinal temporal stability.
