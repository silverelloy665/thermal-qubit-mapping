1. CORE RESEARCH HYPOTHESIS & QUESTION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Research Question:
On a real IBM Heron processor (ibm_marrakesh), does a circuit layout containing a
qubit with high excess excited-state population perform worse than the best layout
that avoids it, within the same local spatial region of the device?

Metric Labeling:
Excess excited-state population is strictly labeled and quantified as:
  "excess P(1|0) over calibrated readout error"
defined as:
  excess_p1 = P(1|0) - readout_error
where P(1|0) is the ground-state reset failure probability measured by unexcited
readout, and readout_error is the independently calibrated measurement error.

3. TOPOLOGICAL NEIGHBORHOOD RADIUS & SUBGRAPH FREEDOM (RADIUS 2 vs RADIUS 4)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Graph Radius Formulation:
Let G = (V, E) be the hardware coupling graph with geodesic graph distance metric
d_G(u, v). The closed radius-R topological ball centered at candidate hot qubit h is:
  B_R(h) = { v in V | d_G(v, h) <= R }

Radius R = 2 Limitation (Why Radius 2 Has No CLEAN Subgraph):
In the IBM Heron heavy-hex architecture, vertex degrees satisfy deg(v) <= 3.
Removing center node h partitions B_2(h) \ {h} into disconnected components C_i
with max_i |C_i| <= 3 < 5. Therefore:
  { S subset (B_2(h) \ {h}) | |S| = 5 and G[S] is connected } = empty_set
It is mathematically impossible for any connected 5-qubit subgraph avoiding h to exist.

Empirical Subgraph Counts at Radius <= 2:
  - Region 1 (h=125): 7 qubits total | HOT 5q subgraphs = 6  | CLEAN 5q subgraphs = 0
  - Region 2 (h=22) : 7 qubits total | HOT 5q subgraphs = 6  | CLEAN 5q subgraphs = 0
  - Region 3 (h=26) : 7 qubits total | HOT 5q subgraphs = 6  | CLEAN 5q subgraphs = 0
  Result: 0 of 3 regions possess a clean connected 5-qubit layout at radius 2.

Radius R <= 4 Expansion (Minimal Connected Neighborhood):
Expanding to R = 4 provides the minimal local topological ball that admits both HOT
and CLEAN connected 5-qubit subgraphs:
  { S subset (B_4(h) \ {h}) | |S| = 5 and G[S] is connected } != empty_set
Empirical counts at radius <= 4:
  - Region 1 (h=125): 19 qubits total | HOT 5q subgraphs = 27 | CLEAN 5q subgraphs = 9
  - Region 2 (h=22) : 16 qubits total | HOT 5q subgraphs = 18 | CLEAN 5q subgraphs = 3
  - Region 3 (h=26) : 19 qubits total | HOT 5q subgraphs = 22 | CLEAN 5q subgraphs = 10

5. CONFOUNDING EXCLUSION CRITERION & DECISION RULE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Confounding Exclusion Formula:
For candidate region r and benchmark B, let hot_2q(r, B) and clean_2q(r, B) denote
the native two-qubit gate counts of the transpiled circuits.
  Delta_2Q(r, B) = clean_2q(r, B) - hot_2q(r, B)
  Cell is CONFOUNDED             <==> |Delta_2Q(r, B)| > 4
  Cell is VALID (non-confounded) <==> |Delta_2Q(r, B)| <= 4

Confounded cells are reported in all tables for transparency, but are strictly
EXCLUDED from contributing to the confirmation decision rule.

Pre-Registered Decision Rule (Per-Region and Benchmark Level):
For region r and benchmark B, let:
  diff(r, B) = success(CLEAN_{r, B}) - success(HOT_{r, B})
and [CI_low(r, B), CI_high(r, B)] be the 95% Newcombe hybrid score confidence interval.

Per-Region Decision Rule:
A candidate region r counts for benchmark B if and only if:
  1. Region is VALID: |Delta_2Q(r, B)| <= 4
  2. CI_low(r, B) > 0
  3. diff(r, B) >= +0.5 pt (+0.0050)
Formula:
  Region r counts for B <==> (|Delta_2Q(r, B)| <= 4) AND (CI_low(r, B) > 0) AND (diff(r, B) >= +0.0050)

Benchmark Confirmation Rule:
Benchmark B is confirmed if and only if at least 2 valid regions count:
  Confirmed(B) <==> sum_{r in Regions} I(Region r counts for B) >= 2

Provisional Feasibility by Benchmark:
  - GHZ-5   : 3 valid regions (R1, R2, R3). Eligible for confirmation (needs >= 2).
  - BV-5    : 2 valid regions (R1, R3). Eligible for confirmation (needs both).
  - Mirror-5: 1 valid region (R1). INELIGIBLE for confirmation (cannot achieve >= 2
              valid regions due to 2Q gate confounding in R2 and R3).
