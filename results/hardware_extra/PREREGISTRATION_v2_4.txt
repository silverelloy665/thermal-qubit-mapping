1. CORE RESEARCH HYPOTHESIS & SCIENTIFIC QUESTION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Research Question:
On an IBM Heron processor (ibm_marrakesh), does a circuit layout containing a
verified persistent high-excess qubit perform worse than the best layout that avoids
it within the same local spatial region of the device?

Metric Labeling & Definition of excess_v2:
Excess excited-state population is strictly labeled and quantified as:
  excess_v2 = P(1|0)_isolated - calibrated prob_meas1_prep0
Fallback Method (when calibrated prob_meas1_prep0 is unexposed by IBM calibration API):
  excess_v2 = P(1|0)_isolated - median_{c in CONTROL}( P(1|0)_isolated,c )
  where CONTROL is the pre-selected set of 8 low-noise qubits with baseline excess <= 0
  and ro <= 0.02 distributed across the chip.
  The excess calculation strictly avoids the symmetric readout error ro.
  Every report and plan artifact explicitly labels which subtraction method was used.

Prior Scientific Caveat:
A qubit with P(1|0) >= 0.20 (such as qubit 125, P10 = 0.42) is expected to degrade
circuit performance trivially due to state-initialization and bit-flip errors. This test
evaluates whether calibration/p1 data reliably predicts that degradation in spatial
routing, NOT whether dilution refrigerator thermal physics is confirmed.

2. AMENDED HOT QUBIT DEFINITION & PERSISTENCE RULE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Unlike the v1 protocol (which relied on a single unrepeated characterization snapshot),
a qubit is classified as HOT under v2.4 if and only if it satisfies the PERSISTENCE RULE:
  1. excess_v2 > 0.05 in BOTH simultaneous register repetitions (Block A rep0 AND rep1).
  2. excess_v2 > 0.05 in graph-isolated measurement (Block B, distance >= 3 from other measured qubits).
  3. Calibrated readout error ro <= 0.20 (excluding pure measurement failures).

Qubits hot in Block A but cold in Block B are classified as "crosstalk-suspect" and
excluded from the hot-spot experiment. If zero qubits pass the persistence rule,
the runner refuses to construct an execution plan; the absence of persistent hot spots
is itself recorded as an empirical finding (Outcome E: ZERO PERSISTENCE).

3. ESP-MATCHED COMPARATORS & LOCAL NEIGHBORHOOD
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
For each candidate region centered at persistent hot qubit h within topological
neighborhood radius R <= 4 (B_4(h)):
  - HOT layout:
      Layout maximizing standard ESP among all connected 5-qubit subgraphs containing h:
      HOT = argmax_{S subset B_4(h), h in S} ESP_std(S)
  - CLEAN_matched comparator:
      CLEAN_matched = argmax_{S subset B_4(h), h not in S} ESP_std(S)
      subject to the constraints:
        1. ESP_std(S) <= ESP_std(HOT)
        2. ESP_std(S) >= ESP_std(HOT) - 0.03
        3. | 2Q_count(S) - 2Q_count(HOT) | <= 4
  - UNMATCHABLE definition:
      If no clean connected 5-qubit subgraph in B_4(h) satisfies both the two-qubit gate
      matching (|Delta 2Q| <= 4) and has ESP_std within 0.03 of ESP_std(HOT), the cell is
      classified as UNMATCHABLE (not executed).
  - Scientific Rationale for ESP Matching:
      Constraining ESP_std(CLEAN_matched) <= ESP_std(HOT) guarantees that a CLEAN win
      cannot be explained by standard ESP (gate fidelity/readout error) advantages.
      The lower bound (within 0.03) ensures the clean layout is not severely handicapped.

4. NEGATIVE CONTROL COMPARATOR (CLEAN2) & CHIP-WIDE SEARCH
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
To establish an empirical within-device layout-variance floor, a negative control layout
(CLEAN2) is evaluated for each executed primary cell:

  - Chip-Wide Candidate Scope (Checkpoint Y / v2.4):
      CLEAN2 candidates are clean connected 5-qubit subgraphs ANYWHERE on the chip (not
      restricted to B_4(h)), ensuring adequate candidate density on large QPUs.
  - Matching Constraints:
      Candidate S must satisfy the identical matching constraints as CLEAN_matched:
        1. ESP_std(S) <= ESP_std(HOT)
        2. ESP_std(S) >= ESP_std(HOT) - 0.03
        3. | 2Q_count(S) - 2Q_count(HOT) | <= 4
  - Spatial Independence & Overlap Rule:
      Candidate S must share at most 2 qubits with CLEAN_matched and at most 2 qubits with HOT:
        |S intersect CLEAN_matched| <= 2  AND  |S intersect HOT| <= 2
  - Selection Ranking:
      Candidates are ranked first by LOWEST total qubit overlap (|S intersect CLEAN_matched| +
      |S intersect HOT|), and ties are broken by HIGHEST standard ESP (ESP_std(S)).
  - Candidate Transparency:
      Every dry-run plan, execution log, and analysis report explicitly prints the number of
      evaluated CLEAN2 candidates for each cell.
  - Countable Cells Definition:
      If a primary cell has a valid CLEAN2 (has_clean2 = True), it is classified as COUNTABLE.
      If no candidate satisfies the matching and overlap constraints (has_clean2 = False),
      the cell is reported but is NOT countable (cannot count toward N or hypothesis confirmation).

5. LAYOUT-VARIANCE FLOOR FORMULA & CELL DECISION RULE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Layout-Variance Floor Formula:
  The layout-variance floor is defined as:
    floor = max( |CLEAN_matched - CLEAN2|, Newcombe_95%_half_width(CLEAN_matched - CLEAN2) )
  where Newcombe_95%_half_width = (ci_high - ci_low) / 2.0.

  Rationale:
  Taking the maximum of the empirical difference and its 95% Newcombe confidence interval
  half-width accounts simultaneously for:
    (a) physical spatial variance between distinct layout embeddings on the lattice, and
    (b) statistical binomial shot-noise uncertainty in estimating the negative control difference.

Cell Decision Rule:
  A countable cell is classified as SIGNIFICANT (positive) if and only if ALL of the following hold:
    1. Newcombe hybrid-score 95% CI lower bound of (CLEAN_matched - HOT) > 0.
    2. Pooled difference Delta_success >= 0.5 percentage points (+0.005).
    3. Repetition sign agreement: Delta_success(rep0) > 0 AND Delta_success(rep1) > 0.
    4. Variance Floor: |CLEAN_matched - HOT| > floor = max(|CLEAN - CLEAN2|, Newcombe_half_width).

  A countable cell is classified as SIGNIFICANT (inverted) if and only if ALL of the following hold:
    1. Newcombe hybrid-score 95% CI upper bound of (CLEAN_matched - HOT) < 0.
    2. Pooled difference Delta_success <= -0.5 percentage points (-0.005).
    3. Repetition sign agreement: Delta_success(rep0) < 0 AND Delta_success(rep1) < 0.
    4. Variance Floor: |CLEAN_matched - HOT| > floor = max(|CLEAN - CLEAN2|, Newcombe_half_width).

6. STUDY STATUS & POWER GATE (COUNTABLE CELLS N)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Study power and outcome classification are defined strictly by count of COUNTABLE cells:
  N = count of executed primary cells that have a valid CLEAN2 comparator.
  P = count of significant-positive countable cells.
  I = count of significant-inverted countable cells.

  - Power Gate:
      Confirmatory hypothesis testing requires N >= 4 countable cells.
  - Unified Status Flag (N < 4):
      If fewer than 4 countable cells exist (N < 4):
        The status flag is: [DESCRIPTIVE, NOT CONFIRMATORY - N < 4].
        Execution proceeds only if --descriptive (or offline --local) is passed.
        All outputs, tables, and CSV provenance are labeled DESCRIPTIVE.
        The outcome CANNOT be declared CONFIRMED under any circumstance.
        Reporting is strictly exploratory and descriptive.

7. IDENTICAL GATES & TRANSPILER PASSES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
All primary circuits (GHZ-5, BV-5, Mirror-5) are compiled under the identical optimization
pipeline (SABRE routing across seeds {0, 1, 2}, optimization_level=3) to native Heron
gates (CZ, RZ, SX, X). Circuit operation sequences are validated prior to execution.

9. GATED LIVE EXECUTION & PILOT SAFETY PROTOCOL
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Shared Pilot Scale File:
   `measure_p1_v2.py --pilot` writes `results/hardware_extra/pilot_scale.json`.
   `run_hotspot_hw.py --execute` reads `results/hardware_extra/pilot_scale.json`.
2. Five Mandatory Pilot Refusal Gates for Live --execute:
   Execution on live QPU strictly refuses if the pilot file:
     (a) has job_id starting with 'pilot_local'
     (b) was generated under --local or --target fake (is_local=True or target != 'live')
     (c) names a backend different from config extra_backend
     (d) lacks a calibration timestamp
     (e) has a timestamp older than 24 hours.
3. Budget Isolation:
   Budget calculations must NEVER use a local pilot scale, defaulting to 3.0 s/PUB until a
   verified live pilot exists.
4. Target Protection:
   Neither script touches a live backend unless `--target live` is explicitly provided.
   Running `--execute` without `--target live` and without `--local` is refused.
5. Priority Step Gating:
   Live execution proceeds in strict priority order:
     Step 1: Pilot (measure_p1_v2.py --pilot --target live)
     Step 2: p1v2 (measure_p1_v2.py --execute --target live)
     Step 3: Hotspot Plan (run_hotspot_hw.py --execute --target live)
   If stored allowance was checked > 24 hours ago, ALL live steps are flagged NO-GO.
   If fresh, each step is GO only if spendable allowance >= step estimate; else NO-GO.
